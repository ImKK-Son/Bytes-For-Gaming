What our buyer-protection score means 🛡️

The cheapest price is not always the safest buy. Every retailer we list gets a buyer-protection score from 1–5 shields based on:

  • Return policy — how easy it is to send something back if it is faulty or wrong.
  • Warranty support — whether you get the full manufacturer warranty and real support.
  • Authenticity — the risk of gray-market or counterfeit units from third-party sellers.
  • Price transparency — no surprise fees, fake "list prices," or bait-and-switch.

That is why, in every comparison, we surface an anti-rip-off pick that balances a fair price with strong protection — not just the lowest number.

Your anti-rip-off checklist ✅

Run through these before you click "buy" on any gaming accessory.

  • Check the seller, not just the site. On marketplaces, make sure it says "Ships from & Sold by" the store itself — not an unknown third-party reseller.
  • Compare against MSRP. A "discount" off a fake inflated price is not a deal. We show the real manufacturer list price so you know the true savings.
  • Beware prices that are too good. A flagship controller at 70% off is usually a scam, a counterfeit, or a gray-market import with no warranty.
  • Confirm the warranty travels with you. Buying direct or from an authorized retailer keeps your factory warranty valid.
  • Read recent reviews for quality drift. Some products get cheaper components over time — check reviews from the last few months.
  • Factor in returns and shipping. A $5 saving is not worth it if returns cost $15 or take a month.
  • Avoid pressure tactics. Fake countdown timers and "only 1 left!" banners exist to rush you. Real deals come back around.
